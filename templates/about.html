About This Project

A machine learning–powered traffic volume prediction system developed for Civil Engineering Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS).

🚦 Project Overview

The Traffic Volume Prediction System uses historical traffic and meteorological data to predict the hourly traffic volume on a major interstate highway corridor. By combining environmental features (temperature, rainfall, snowfall, cloud cover) with cyclical temporal patterns (hour, day of week, month, holidays), the system provides accurate traffic flow estimates that directly support transportation planning and operational engineering decisions.

This tool is designed for transportation engineers, urban planners, and ITS researchers who need data-driven insights into roadway demand for pavement design, signal timing optimization, and capacity management.

📂 Dataset

The project uses the Metro Interstate Traffic Volume dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository (ID 492) .

  • Source Agency: Minnesota Department of Transportation (MnDOT)
  • Location: Interstate 94 Westbound (Milepost 241, ATR Station 301), Minneapolis–St. Paul, MN
  • Observation Span: 2012 – 2018
  • Total Instances: 48,204 hourly observations
  • Target Variable: Traffic volume (vehicles/hour)
  • Key Features: Temperature, rain, snow, cloud cover, holiday status, timestamp features

🤖 Machine Learning Model

The primary deployed prediction model is built using Multiple Linear Regression with a StandardScaler preprocessing pipeline in scikit-learn, enhanced by trigonometric cyclical feature encoding.

  • Algorithm: Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Linear Regression with Cyclical Feature Mapping
  • Framework: scikit-learn & joblib
  • Data Splitting: 80% Chronological Training / 20% Holdout Testing (No Shuffling to prevent lookahead data leakage)
  • Evaluation Metrics: Mean Absolute Error (MAE), Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE), Coefficient of Determination (R²)
  • Feature Engineering: Cyclical sine and cosine transformations of hour, day-of-week, and month

🏗️ Civil Engineering Applications

Accurate traffic volume prediction is critical for numerous aspects of transportation infrastructure and ITS:

🛣️ Road Design & Capacity Planning

Determine required lane configuration, geometric design, and structural pavement thickness based on forecasted vehicle loads.

🚦 Signal Timing & Ramp Metering

Calibrate adaptive traffic signal timing plans and expressway ramp meter rates to prevent breakdown of mainline traffic flow.

📋 Transportation Demand Management

Predict periods of recurring congestion to support dynamic toll pricing, transit dispatch, and high-occupancy lane policies.

🔧 Work Zone & Maintenance Scheduling

Schedule lane closures, resurfacing, and bridge inspections during minimum-volume windows to minimize commuter disruption.

🌍 Environmental Impact Analysis

Estimate vehicle tailpipe emissions, greenhouse gas outputs, and noise pollution contours for urban environmental audits.

💰 Economic Feasibility Studies

Quantify travel time savings, vehicle operating cost reductions, and benefit-cost ratios for proposed highway expansions.
